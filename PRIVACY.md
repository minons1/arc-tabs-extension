# Privacy Policy — Arc Tabs

**Last updated:** June 28, 2026

Arc Tabs is a browser extension that helps you manage inactive tabs. This policy explains what data we handle and how.

## What we store

Arc Tabs stores the following data **locally in your browser** (via `chrome.storage.local`):

- **Tab metadata** — URL, domain, title, and last active timestamp for open tabs
- **Protected domains** — domains you've marked as "keep" (never auto-close)
- **Settings** — your inactivity threshold, check interval, accent color, and operating mode

## What we don't do

- ❌ We do **not** collect, transmit, or sell any personal data
- ❌ We do **not** send data to any external server or third party
- ❌ We do **not** use analytics, tracking, or cookies
- ❌ We do **not** access, read, or store page content — only tab URLs and titles

## Data retention

All data is stored locally and persists until you remove the extension. Uninstalling Arc Tabs deletes all stored data automatically.

## Your control

You can delete your data at any time by:
- Removing individual protected domains in the Protected tab
- Uninstalling the extension (deletes everything)

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
